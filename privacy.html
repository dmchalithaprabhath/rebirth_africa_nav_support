Privacy Policy

Rebirth Africa Nav

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Rebirth Africa Nav (“the app”) handles your information. We keep it simple and transparent.

Location Data

The app uses your device’s location to show your position on the map and to provide navigation (including turn-by-turn directions). Location is used only while you use the app and only for these features. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings at any time.

Data Stored on Your Device

Offline map data, saved locations, and preferences are stored on your device. This data is not sent to our servers unless the app explicitly syncs or backs up data to an account (if that feature is used).

Account Information

If you sign in or create an account, we collect only what is needed to provide the service (e.g. email, account identifier). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The app may use map tiles or routing services. Use of the app may involve standard internet communication (e.g. HTTPS). We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

For questions or requests about your data, contact us at dmchalitha@gmail.com.
